# Myuuel character guidelines

Myuuel is SparkleSnap's character. The **code** in this repository is MIT-licensed (see [LICENSE](LICENSE)), but Myuuel herself — her design, artwork, sprite sheets and animation — is not part of that licence. Instead, she is shared under these guidelines, in the spirit of characters like Hatsune Miku and the Touhou cast: **you are welcome to use her, as long as you credit her home and treat her kindly.**

## What these guidelines cover

- The Myuuel character: her name, look and personality.
- Her artwork and animation in `assets/myuuel/`, and the illustrations in `docs/media/`.

The SparkleSnap name and logos (`assets/brand/`) are covered separately [below](#the-sparklesnap-brand).

## You may — freely, without asking

- **Make fan works.** Draw her, animate her, cosplay her, write about her, put her in videos, streams, games and mods.
- **Use this app's art in your fork or remix** of Myuuel Memory Practice, or in another free, non-commercial project.
- **Share** what you make, online or in print, for free.

## Please always

- **Credit her home.** For example: *Myuuel © SparkleSnap — sparklesnap.dev*. In a video description, a README, an About screen or the end of a page is fine.
- **Make it clear it's yours.** A fan work or fork should not look like an official SparkleSnap release. Call it fan-made, a fork or a remix, and give it a name of your own.
- **Keep her kind.** Myuuel is a calm, friendly companion, and anyone of any age should be able to meet her.

## Please don't

- Put her in sexual, hateful, violent or shocking content.
- Use her for political campaigns, scams, phishing or anything meant to deceive, including pages that pretend to be SparkleSnap.
- Claim her as your own character, or register her name or look as a trademark, design or domain.
- Sell her, or products featuring her, without asking first. This includes merchandise, paid apps, NFTs and stock-asset packs.

## Want to do something else?

Commercial use, merchandise, collaborations or anything not covered here: just ask via [sparklesnap.dev](https://sparklesnap.dev/). Most requests are welcome.

## The SparkleSnap brand

The SparkleSnap name, watermark and logos may only be used to credit or link to SparkleSnap. Please don't use them as the branding of your own project. In a fork, replace `assets/brand/` with your own mark.

SparkleSnap may update these guidelines, and can ask for a specific use to stop if it goes against their spirit. (^.^)
